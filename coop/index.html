How it works

The way a cooperative actually runs — just no longer on paper.

1

Members join

Add existing members in bulk from a spreadsheet, or one at a time. Each gets their own record — no more shared exercise books.

2

They save, on a schedule

Set contribution targets and watch progress in real time. Members pay by bank transfer, card, or in person — every kobo lands on the right record automatically.

3

They borrow against it

Loan applications, guarantor approval, and disbursement — all tracked, with a real repayment schedule generated the moment money goes out.

4

Dues stay current

Membership dues accrue automatically and can block a loan application until they're cleared — your rules, enforced without anyone having to remember them.

5

Everyone stays informed

Send a reminder or an announcement to one member or the whole society. No more relying on word of mouth.
