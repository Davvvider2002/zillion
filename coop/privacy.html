Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026
This document is a plain-language starting point, not a substitute for legal advice. Before real member data is handled under it, it should be reviewed by a qualified lawyer for full alignment with Nigeria's Data Protection Act and Data Protection Regulation.

1. What this covers

This policy explains what personal information Zillion Coop collects from Societies and their members, why, and how it's handled. It applies to the web portal, coop-flavored wallet app, and related backend services.

2. Information we collect

FromWhatWhy
Society adminsName, phone, email, society name, locationAccount setup, billing, support
MembersName, phone number, opening balance (if any)Recording membership, savings, dues, and loans
PaymentsBank account details, transaction referencesProcessing dues, savings, and loan repayments via our payment processor
DeviceDevice identifier, IP address, general usage logsSecurity, fraud prevention, and troubleshooting

3. How we use this information

  • To operate the core features of the platform — recording savings, dues, loans, and generating accounting reports for a Society.
  • To verify identity at signup and login (phone or email one-time codes).
  • To process payments through our payment partner, Flutterwave.
  • To send account-related notifications — trial reminders, payment confirmations, and service alerts — by SMS or email.
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse of the platform.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. It is shared only with:

  • The member's own Society — a Society's administrators can see their own members' records, since that's the core purpose of the platform.
  • Service providers we use to operate the platform: Flutterwave (payments), our SMS provider, and our transactional email provider — each only receives what they need to perform their specific function.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, only where required by law.

5. Data storage and security

Data is stored on secure, access-controlled infrastructure. Passwords and one-time codes are never stored in plain text. Access to a Society's data is restricted to that Society's own administrators and Zillion's own operations team where needed for support.

6. How long we keep it

Member and financial records are kept for as long as a Society remains active on the platform, and for a reasonable period afterward to meet standard financial record-keeping practice. A Society or member can request deletion of their data, subject to any records we're legally required to retain.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Requests can be sent to support@zillion.ng.

8. Children

Zillion Coop is intended for use by adults managing cooperative society finances. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests relating to your personal data, can be sent to support@zillion.ng.
