Delete your account

Use this page to ask us to delete your Zillion Coop account and personal data. You do not need to sign in.

What happens next

  • We call the phone number you give here, to confirm it is really you.
  • Once confirmed, we close your account and delete your personal details, such as your name, email, address and ID details, within 30 days.
  • You can also choose to keep your account and have only some of your data removed.

What we may have to keep

Financial records, such as savings contributions, loan and repayment entries, and society accounting records, must be kept for the period the law and your society's books require. Where we keep them, they are cut off from your personal details as far as the law allows and are used only for legal, tax and audit purposes. If you have an unpaid loan or are a guarantor on one, your society will need to settle it first.

You can also email support@zillion.ng from the email address on your account.
